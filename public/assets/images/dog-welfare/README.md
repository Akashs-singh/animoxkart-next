## Image Guidelines

### Photography Style
- **Authentic:** Real moments, real people, real dogs
- **Documentary:** Not posed, capture genuine interactions
- **Natural Tones:** Earthy colors, avoid bright/flashy edits
- **Raw & Real:** Slight imperfections acceptable and preferred
- **Indian Context:** Local environments, culturally appropriate
- **Grain:** Natural film grain acceptable for authenticity
- **No Stock Photos:** Avoid generic or AI-generated imagery
- **Honest:** Show reality without being manipulative or guilt-heavy

### Technical Requirements
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Quality:** High resolution, web-optimized
- **File Size:** Under 500KB per image (compressed)
- **Color Space:** sRGB
- **Aspect Ratios:**
  - Hero: 16:9 (landscape)
  - Reality: 4:3 (landscape)
  - Moments: 3:4 (portrait)

### Emotional Tone
- **Honest:** Show reality as it is
- **Hopeful:** Not sad or guilt-driven
- **Grounded:** Real situations, real impact
- **Compassionate:** Show care and dignity
- **Human:** Focus on connection between people and dogs

### What to Avoid
- Overly sad or dramatic imagery
- Guilt-inducing photos
- Stock photo aesthetics
- AI-generated or fake-looking images
- Overly polished or corporate style
- Bright, flashy colors
- Staged or posed shots

## Photography Tips

If capturing original photos:
- Shoot during golden hour (early morning/late afternoon)
- Use natural lighting
- Capture candid moments
- Show respect and dignity
- Focus on human-animal connection
- Include environmental context
- Avoid flash photography (can scare dogs)
- Get permission from people in photos
- Show diversity in volunteers and settings
