## Image Guidelines

### Photography Style
- **Authentic:** Real people, real workspace, real moments
- **Documentary:** Not staged or overly posed
- **Warm Tones:** Beige, brown, soft orange color palette
- **Natural Lighting:** Soft, warm lighting preferred
- **Grain:** Slight film grain acceptable for realism
- **No Stock Photos:** Avoid generic stock imagery
- **Indian Context:** Culturally appropriate, Indian faces and environments

### Technical Requirements
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Quality:** High resolution, optimized for web
- **File Size:** Under 500KB per image (optimized)
- **Color Space:** sRGB
- **Aspect Ratios:**
  - Hero: 16:9 (landscape)
  - Stories: 1:1 (square)
  - Behind Scenes: 3:4 (portrait)

### Emotional Tone
- **Human:** Show real emotions, not forced smiles
- **Honest:** Authentic work environment
- **Empowering:** Show dignity and pride in work
- **Trustworthy:** Build connection with viewer
